## 🎮 What It Is
BetterJoy for Windows is a free tool that makes Nintendo Switch controllers work naturally on a PC. Pair a Joy-Con or Pro Controller and BetterJoy presents it to Windows as an Xbox controller, so your games pick it up without any special setup. Gyro aiming, rumble, and Joy-Con combining all work, and you can remap buttons or calibrate sticks to taste. Open-source and community-maintained on GitHub, this BetterJoy download is the easy way to bring your Switch controllers to PC gaming.

## ⭐ Key Features
> A free driver that makes Switch controllers feel at home on your PC.

- 🕹️ **Joy-Con and Pro Controller** — Use them both on Windows.
- 🎯 **Xbox emulation** — Games see a standard Xbox controller.
- 🌀 **Gyro and motion** — Enable tilt and motion aiming where supported.
- 📳 **Rumble** — Feel vibration feedback in your hands.
- 🔧 **Remap and calibrate** — Adjust buttons and sticks to fit you.
